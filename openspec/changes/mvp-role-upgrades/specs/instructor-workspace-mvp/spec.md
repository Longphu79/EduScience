## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Instructor overview surfaces operational priorities
The instructor workspace SHALL summarize publication pipeline, payout readiness, learner engagement, and next actions.

#### Scenario: Instructor opens overview
- **WHEN** an authenticated instructor opens the instructor overview route
- **THEN** the page SHALL show live summary metrics
- **AND** SHALL highlight publication and payout follow-up work

### Requirement: Instructor workspace links to revenue and course management
The instructor workspace SHALL keep payout and course management accessible from the operational overview.

#### Scenario: Instructor needs to act on a course or payout issue
- **WHEN** the overview detects draft courses, missing payout readiness, or recent student comments
- **THEN** the page SHALL provide direct navigation to the relevant workspace route
